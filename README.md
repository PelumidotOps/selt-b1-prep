# SELT B2 Communicator Simulator (LanguageCert UKVI)

An interactive, continuous-practice study web application specifically built for candidates preparing for the **LanguageCert International ESOL SELT B2 (Communicator) 4-Skills Exam** for UK Visa applications (Health & Care Worker, Skilled Worker, and Student routes).

## ✨ Key Features & B2 Standards

- **🔄 Continuous Dynamic Question Engine:**
  - Automated question advancement and score tracking for Listening and Reading.
  - Interactive validation with in-depth explanations of LanguageCert distractor traps.
  - Unlimited practice with "Next Question" and "New Mock" generators.

- **🎙️ Speaking Simulator (13–15 Mins Official B2 Structure):**
  - **Part 1 (Abstract Opinions):** Questions on technology, work-life balance, lifelong learning, and professional ethics.
  - **Part 2 (Roleplays):** Two high-stakes professional & social scenarios (negotiations, complaints, duty conflicts).
  - **Part 3 (Collaborative Discussion):** Task prompt with 4 options to evaluate pros/cons and negotiate consensus.
  - **Part 4 (Long Turn):** **30s preparation** followed by a full **2-minute uninterrupted speech timer** (120s circular countdown gauge) + 2 examiner follow-up questions.
  - Built-in browser audio recorder for speech self-evaluation.

- **✍️ Writing Studio (B2 Word Counts & Discourse Markers):**
  - **Task 1 (100–150 words):** Formal response (letters, proposals, and official emails).
  - **Task 2 (150–200 words):** Discursive compositions and opinion articles.
  - Live B2 discourse markers tracker (*consequently, nevertheless, whereas, in spite of, on the contrary, notably*).
  - High-scoring preloaded B2 templates.

- **🎧 Listening Simulator (26 Questions Standard):**
  - Synthesized British audio dialogue player.
  - Drills on Response Prediction, Attitude & Hidden Intent, and Multi-Speaker Debates.

- **📖 Reading Simulator (Sentence Gaps & Matching):**
  - Authentic **Part 2 Gapped Sentences** drills.
  - **Part 3 Multi-Text Matching** across 4 professional viewpoints.

- **🎨 Design:**
  - Built with the Agentix Brand identity (Electric Orange `#FF7A00`, Geist/Inter fonts, grid-pattern, and floating mobile dock).
